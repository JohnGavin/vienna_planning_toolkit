Plan where the outlets, switches and lights of a flat go, on top of the architect's drawing. Your plan file is read in this browser and never sent anywhere.

The electrical editor

  • One tab per storey of the plan.
  • Drag a symbol from the palette onto the drawing, or click it, then click the drawing.
  • Faint rings show where the rules suggest a symbol; let go near one and it snaps on.
  • Rings are suggestions only: they are not placed and do not print.
  • Connect: click a switch, then the lights it works; the page proposes the switch type.
  • Layout checks under the drawing: lights, switches, sockets, kitchen, smoke alarms, symbols outside rooms.
  • Keep your work as a layout file (Save or Copy layout); Open layout brings it back.

Quick start

  1. Look at the example: the sample flat opens with one room planned.
  2. Drag a symbol from the Symbols palette onto the drawing.
  3. Connect: click a switch, then each light it works.
  4. Keep your work: Save layout (Pages) or Copy layout (demo).
  5. Print view → ⌘P / Ctrl+P → Save as PDF (Pages version).

How to

One tab per task: the steps first, the details folded below.

  1. Drag a symbol from the Symbols palette onto the drawing; let go where it belongs.
  2. Or click it in the palette, then click the drawing: picking alone places nothing.
  3. Rings are suggestions: let go near one and the symbol snaps onto it.
  4. Escape cancels a picked symbol; zoom and pan never move a placed one.
▶Symbol palette
  • The chosen set's symbols, grouped by category, each with its German and English name.
  • Drag one onto the drawing (mouse or finger).
  • Or click it (it stays marked), then click the drawing; Escape cancels.
  • The palette folds away with its title.
▶Placing a symbol
  • Drag and let go: the insertion point lands where you let go.
  • Or click the symbol in the palette, then click the drawing.
  • Picked is not placed: until you click the drawing, the rings are only suggestions.
  • Keyboard: Enter on the symbol, then Enter on the drawing places it mid-view.
  • Stored in drawing (model) coordinates: zoom and pan never move it.
  • The same position a DXF export will use.
▶Room of a symbol
  • Each placed symbol gets the id of its room from the plan file.
  • Inside a room's outline: that room.
  • Else the nearest label of a room without an outline, if close enough.
  • Else could not tell.
  • Shown when the symbol is selected; saved in the layout file.
  • room_label_max_m2 m
  • symbol_scale1 × nominal mm
  1. Click a placed symbol to select it (dashed box); drag it to move it.
  2. Rotate 90° (key R) turns it; a socket's stem points to its wall.
  3. Delete (key Delete) removes the symbol or the selected link.
  4. Undo / Redo (Ctrl/⌘ + Z) take any change back.
▶Select and move
  • Click a placed symbol to select it (dashed box); drag to move it.
  • Click the empty drawing to deselect.
  • Dragging the empty drawing pans the view.
▶Rotate 90°
  • Turns the selected symbol 90° counter-clockwise about its insertion point (key R).
  • A socket's stem points to the wall it sits on: rotate until it does.
▶Delete
  • Deletes the selected symbol with its links, or the selected link.
  • Keys: Delete or Backspace. Undo brings it back.
▶Undo / Redo
  • Undo takes back the last change: place, move, rotate, delete, connect, apply, open.
  • Redo does it again.
  • Keys: Ctrl/⌘ + Z and Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Z.
  • The history is kept until the page is closed.
  1. Click Connect: the switches are marked, everything else is dimmed.
  2. Click a switch: what it may control turns green.
  3. Click each light it works: a dashed link is drawn.
  4. Escape or Connect again stops.
▶Connect (switch to light)
  • Connect turns Connect mode on or off; symbols do not move in it.
  • Switches are marked; everything else is dimmed.
  • Click a switch: what it may control is marked green.
  • Click each light it works: a dashed link is drawn on Elektro – Verbindungen.
  • A light it may not control is refused; the message names what it may control.
  • Another switch starts again; the same switch or Escape stops.
▶Links (Elektro – Verbindungen)
  • Dashed lines between switch and light insertion points: not cable routes.
  • Untick the layer to hide them on screen and in print.
  • Click a line to select it (thicker); Delete removes it, Undo brings it back.
  • Deleting a symbol deletes its links too.
  1. Switching groups (under the drawing) lists the lights that share switches.
  2. The page proposes the type: 1 Ausschaltung, 2 Wechselschaltung, 3+ Kreuzschaltung.
  3. Apply swaps the placed switches to the proposal; nothing changes without it.
▶Switching groups and the proposed switch type
  • A group is a set of lights sharing switches (joined by links).
  • 1 switch: Ausschaltung (one-way switch).
  • 2 switches: Wechselschaltung (two two-way switches).
  • 3 or more: Kreuzschaltung (two two-way switches, an intermediate switch per extra place).
  • With a push-button: no proposal (impulse relay or timer: the Elektroplaner decides).
  • Only shown: nothing changes until you click Apply.
  • The textbook rule: the actual wiring is for the Elektroplaner.
▶Apply the proposal
  • Swaps this group's switch symbols to the proposed codes.
  • Positions, rooms and links stay.
  • One click, one Undo step.
  1. While you pick, drag or move a symbol, rings show the suggested spots.
  2. The ring it will snap to turns yellow; let go near it.
  3. Untick Snap, or hold Alt/Option, to place freely.
  4. Rules picks the values: starting values (to confirm) or the Elektroplaner's.
  5. Layout checks under the drawing say what is ok, to look at or could not tell.

  • The ring it would snap to is yellow; rings keep their size at every zoom.

About

Privacy, the two versions of this page, what is new, how it is built.

  • One self-contained page: no server, no analytics, nothing loaded from other sites.
  • Files you open or paste are read by this browser only.
  • Drafts stay in this browser's storage.
  • tools/export_plan.py runs on your computer and writes the plan file there.
  • The plan file holds the DXF's name and checksum, not its folder.
  • Same code, two pages (tools/build_editor.py).
  • GitHub Pages version: real work: Save layout, Save to file, Save parameters, Print view.
  • Shareable demo (claude.ai artifact): no download, save or print; Copy instead.
  • Both open a plan, layout or parameter file, or accept its text by paste.
  • Work does not move between them by itself: copy the layout, paste it in the other.
